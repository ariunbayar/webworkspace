Left, and running now: real X windows, watched but never owned. The page asks xprop and xdotool what exists, and photographs a workspace whenever you visit it — X only hands out the pixels on the glass, so a workspace you are not looking at cannot be captured.

Right: shells this page starts itself. pty_server.py holds them, so it knows their pid, working directory and what is running in them outright rather than guessing from WM_CLASS, and they survive a reload — the tab only holds the emulator drawing one. Sessions arrive in the same windows[] list as synthetic entries, in a cell of their own past the last workspace, which is what lets them share the surface with real windows.
